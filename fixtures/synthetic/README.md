# Synthetic fixtures

All files here are generated by `scripts/make_fixtures.py` with invented names and content. No real people, no real chats. Use them for demos, tests and bug reports.

The three paste fixtures tell three different stories on purpose: in `warm` the other person accepts invitations and settles on times, in `cooling` they re-schedule and deflect, and `stop_contact` ends on an explicit request to stop. They are generated with a fixed seed, but **changing the generator changes the material** — numbers in any report written before that change are not comparable with numbers after it.
